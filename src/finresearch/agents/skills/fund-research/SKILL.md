# Mutual-fund research playbook

## Sources, strongest first
1. AMFI: daily NAVs and NAV history (tools), TER disclosures.
2. The AMC's own documents: SID/KIM, monthly factsheet, monthly portfolio disclosure (cite URL, document date and
   access time).
3. SEBI circulars for category rules; credible press for events.

## What good fund research checks
- **Same plan and option everywhere:** compare direct-growth with direct-growth. Regular plans carry commission.
- **Point-to-point returns flatter or punish the end date:** always show rolling returns too.
- **Category rank on several horizons**, and whether the fund stays within its SEBI mandate (market-cap bands).
- **Costs compound:** TER differences matter over 10+ years; exit loads matter for short holding periods.
- **Risk-adjusted returns need a stated risk-free rate** (source and date).
- **Manager tenure:** a strong record under a manager who has left says little about the future.

## Mistakes to avoid
- Mixing IDCW and growth NAVs, or annualising periods under a year without saying so.
- Using a factsheet figure without its "as of" date.
- Treating past returns as a forecast.
