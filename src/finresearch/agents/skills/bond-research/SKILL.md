# Listed bond / NCD research playbook

## Sources, strongest first
1. The offer document (shelf prospectus and tranche document, or information memorandum): coupon, frequency,
   redemption schedule, options, security.
2. Rating agency press releases (instrument-level rating, outlook, drivers and sensitivities).
3. Issuer filings (annual report, results, ALM for NBFCs) and NSE listing data.

## What good bond research checks
- **Terms before yield:** a YTM computed with the wrong frequency or ignoring partial redemptions is wrong.
- **Clean vs dirty price:** know whether the quoted price includes accrued interest; say which you used.
- **Spread for risk:** compare YTM with a G-sec of similar maturity and with bonds of the same rating band.
- **Liquidity:** listed does not mean liquid; look at traded value and trading days.
- **Post-tax yield:** interest is taxed at the investor's slab rate unless the bond is tax-free.
- **Credit trajectory:** rating history and "issuer not cooperating" flags matter more than today's letter grade.
